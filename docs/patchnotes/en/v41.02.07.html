Camera: free camera after leaving Sku standard

  • When Sku standard is released, your own camera settings are now kept -

even when turning with T or toward a waypoint. The forced camera snap (SetView) only applies in Sku standard. Turning itself still works.

  • Personal camera profile: changes made in free mode are saved and

automatically restored after login. Safe Sku standard remains the default for everyone else.

  • Menu 7 "Video options" as the basis for the new structure.

Version

  • Sku.toc and SkuDispatcher/Sku.toc updated to v41.02.07.
